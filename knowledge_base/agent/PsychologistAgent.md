# Psychologist

## Description

Represents a psychologist, therapist, counsellor, or mental health professional involved in child protection.

Unlike teachers, psychologists focus on:

- emotional wellbeing
- mental health
- resilience development
- behavioural assessment
- trauma-informed intervention
- long-term outcomes

The psychologist does not typically observe the child continuously.

Instead, the psychologist receives information from:

- the child
- parents
- teachers
- the protection system
- case reports
- previous interventions

The psychologist may participate as an expert whose recommendations influence intervention selection. 【1-2b3352】
