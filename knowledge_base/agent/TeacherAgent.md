# Teacher

## Description

Represents a teacher or educational professional interacting with the child.

The objective of the teacher is not law enforcement or platform moderation.

The teacher primarily contributes through:

- support
- guidance
- observation
- safeguarding
- referral to appropriate services

A teacher may or may not be aware of incidents occurring outside school.
