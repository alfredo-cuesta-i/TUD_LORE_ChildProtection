# Platform
## Description
Represents the online platform where interactions occur.
The platform is not necessarily malicious or benevolent.
The platform balances multiple competing goals:
- user safety
- user engagement
- legal compliance
- reputation protection
- cost minimization
- business sustainability
Different platform profiles may lead to very different outcomes even when children, parents and teachers remain identical.
